Quick start

  1. Classify one image. Open Classify, drop a satellite tile or click a test image. You get the land-use class, confidence and a Grad-CAM heatmap.
  2. Give feedback. Tell the system whether it was right. Corrections feed the live-accuracy figure on the Monitoring page.
  3. Process many images. Use Batch, then sort, filter and export the results as CSV.
  4. Watch the model in production. On Monitoring, press Simulate drifted requests to see the drift alarm fire.
  5. Review the model. Model report shows test accuracy, per-class scores and evaluation figures.

What the numbers mean

ConfidenceThe model’s probability for its top class. Below 60% is flagged for human review.
Grad-CAMHeatmap of the image regions that drove the prediction (red = most important).
PSIPopulation Stability Index: how different recent images are from the training data. < 0.10 stable, 0.10–0.25 warning, > 0.25 alert.
Macro-F1Average F1 over the 10 classes, so every class counts equally.
